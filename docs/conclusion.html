Chapter 8 Conclusion

The surge in global military spending dominating today’s headlines is not just a defense policy story it is a development story, and it speaks directly to the ambitions laid out in both Sustainable Development Goal 8 and Sustainable Development Goal 16.

SDG 8 calls for sustained, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth, full employment, and decent work for all. When the United States proposes a 1.5 trillion dollar defense budget represting a large budget and the economic effects are significant. On one hand, defense spending can show certain sectors of the economy, creating jobs in manufacturing, technology, and logistics. On the other hand, when such spending is financed through increases as analysts warn this latest proposal would add nearly $6 trillion to the national debt over a decade. This would be a long-term economic consequences can undermine the very growth and stability SDG 8 is designed to protect.

The connection to SDG 16 is perhaps even more direct. SDG 16 envisions governments that are accountable, transparent, and oriented toward justice and inclusive institutions. The current debate over military spending raises serious questions about all three.When massive amounts of money go to secret programs, when normal voting rules are bypassed to push spending bills through, and when cuts to programs that help everyday people are made to pay for it, the kind of open and fair government that SDG 16 calls for becomes harder to achieve.Accountbilty for a government is not just about preventing corruption but ensuring that the public money serves the public good in a way that people can see, understand, and partipate in shaping a world where everything is transparent. The current moment in milltary spending illustrates a tension within both goals. A Government can pursue strength while not having the foundations for economic stability and integrity that its own citzens demand. SDG 8 and 16 together remind us that national is not to be measured by defense budgets and weapon systems but a heathy economy that provides fairness in any capacity to its people.
